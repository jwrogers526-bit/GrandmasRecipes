Southern Grilled Cheeseburgers

Like a backyard burger and a grilled cheese had a delicious idea.

Golden Texas toast, a juicy burger patty, two kinds of cheese, and a tangy sauce—this one is best eaten with extra napkins.

Prep time15 minutes
Cook time15 minutes
Servings4
Jump to the recipe ↓All recipes
From the recipe box

A diner-style lunch at home.

Read through the steps once before you start, and have your ingredients ready to go.

What you'll need

  • 1½ lb ground beef (80/20 works well)
  • 8 thick slices Texas toast
  • 8 slices sharp cheddar or American cheese
  • 2 tbsp softened butter
  • 1 tsp kosher salt; ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • 4 lettuce leaves and 1 tomato, sliced
  • Pickles, optional
  • ¼ cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tbsp mustard
  • 1 tsp pickle juice or apple cider vinegar

Let's make it

  1. Make the sauce: Stir mayonnaise, ketchup, mustard, and pickle juice together. Refrigerate while you cook.
  2. Shape the patties: Divide beef into four equal portions; form patties slightly wider than the toast. Season both sides with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
  3. Cook the burgers: Heat a skillet or griddle over medium-high heat. Cook patties about 4–5 minutes per side, adjusting for thickness, until the center reaches 160°F.
  4. Melt the cheese: Place a slice of cheese over each patty for the last minute of cooking; cover briefly until melted.
  5. Toast the bread: Lightly butter the outside of the Texas toast. In a clean skillet, toast until golden, flipping as needed. Add another cheese slice to the bread while warm if you like it extra cheesy.
  6. Build the burgers: Spread sauce on the toast, then layer lettuce, tomato, patty, and pickles. Top with another slice of toast and serve immediately.

Grandma's kitchen tip

Don’t press the patties down with a spatula while they cook; you’ll squeeze out the juices. If your toast is especially thick, let it toast over medium heat so the center warms before the outside browns.

Lunch tastes better with a little extra cheese.
